# Proposal

## Why

Piarre needs evidence that a local orchestrator can clarify consequential uncertainty, ground answers, and propose appropriate tools within practical device limits. Model size and published coding scores do not answer that question.

## What Changes

- Specify an eligibility queue containing every issue candidate and the required pinned Qwen baseline. Admit at most one challenger to a later trial after review.
- Define recorded-tool cases for clarification, grounding, routing, correction/recovery, and efficiency/usefulness accounting. Preserve optional clarification skip with visible provisional assumptions and permission checks.
- Reuse #104's compatibility validator and Bonsai row, #94's runner/scorer, #102's cost contract, #96's clarification semantics, and #86's authority boundaries.
- Keep ToolOrchestra/Nemotron-Orchestrator research-only and excluded from production selection pending rights. Critical authority or isolation failures veto promotion.
- Publish the plan and wait for human review. No downloads, inference, hosted escalation, installer, ensemble, or model-default change follows from this proposal.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `orchestrator-comparison`: A source-labeled candidate queue and matched evidence contract for local orchestrator decisions.

### Modified Capabilities

None. These proposed experiment requirements do not accept new product behavior or modify a main spec.

## Impact

Later offline fixtures would extend #94's PR #95 benchmark instead of adding a platform. PR #89's bounded Qwen route remains an unmerged baseline dependency. Actual model quality and expert-handoff trials remain paused behind #100/#91 and a separately approved execution protocol. Refs #105.
