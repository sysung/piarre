# Proposal

## Why

Refs #102. Lower token usage does not establish lower cost per accepted coding task when preparation, failed attempts, review, and reconciliation are omitted. A fair paired protocol must separate context selection, local preparation, and orchestration effects while preserving quality and security.

## What Changes

- Propose a bounded #94 accounting/protocol extension for direct Codex A, deterministic Piarre handoff B, local Qwen preparation C, and a direct matched-evidence control.
- Specify twelve eligibility slots across two repository families. These slots are not authored or frozen tasks; independent task and holdout creation remains future work.
- Preserve all-attempt usage, provider/subscription distinctions, reviewer/coordinator overhead, failure outcomes, and undefined cost per accepted task at zero successes.
- Keep output filtering, skills, routing, caching, parallelism, and programmatic calls as separately controlled follow-ups. No combined optimization campaign starts here.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `coding-method-evaluation`: Comparable coding-method manifests and honest all-attempt acceptance/cost reports.

### Modified Capabilities

None. This planning root has no main specs. These proposed experiment requirements do not establish product readiness or model-quality results.

## Impact

Future offline work extends #94's existing benchmark in open PR #95, not a second platform. #90 owns caching, #86 tool programs, #83 instructions, and #79/#93 retrieval. #100/#91's pilot remains the priority. Plan publication is followed by human review before Apply. Campaign spend is proposed at zero until the pilot pause is explicitly revisited and the route, scope, isolation, resource limits, and budget are separately approved. No coding agents, worktree executions, inference, or paid runs occur in this phase.
