# Legacy documentation disposition

The target documents come from sysung/piarre at `8a8b7ab38d9cb0d272389ef89a089e5cee9b7832`. [The source manifest](source-manifest.json) identifies each copy by path, revision, and SHA-256. Line numbers refer to those copies. Headings are grouped with their following text. Every nonblank line has a row. The snapshots preserve the full paragraphs even when the current destination covers only part of the scope.

- **Contract** identifies a narrower existing capability or automated acceptance contract. Tests and baseline sources establish the recorded scope.
- **Context** identifies current explanatory prose, user instructions, or owner design guidance.
- **Open** identifies a pending criterion or an unresolved design retained in the named change or source passage.
- **Superseded** identifies an authority or navigation instruction replaced by the migration.
- **History** identifies a preserved source, proposal, experiment, or observation with its original limits.

[Migration decisions D3, D4, D6, and D7](../2026-11-13-055759-consolidate-project-documentation/design.md#decisions) establish documentation authority, preservation, acceptance resolution, and verification placement. [Pending criteria](../../2026-11-13-061200-retained-delivery-scope/design.md) and [imported automated contracts](../../../specs/imported-acceptance-baseline/spec.md) preserve the catalog coverage. [Catalog coverage](catalog-coverage.md) records the exact comparison.
